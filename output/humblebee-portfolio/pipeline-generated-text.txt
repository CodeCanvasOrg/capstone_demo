  HumbleBeeAI | Applied AI, built around real workflows  Week 8 review draft • 5 November 2026  Executive Summary  HumbleBeeAI’s public portfolio brings together applied AI delivery, products and research across document intelligence, computer vision, analytics and AI platforms. This client-facing review organizes 25 case studies, eight products, seven papers and two company updates; results are attributed to published materials, not independently audited, and product labels reproduce the supplied PDF snapshots.   Company Overview  HumbleBeeAI builds document intelligence, computer vision, analytics and AI platforms for operational teams. Its published work spans customs and logistics, retail, healthcare, workforce systems and interactive AI products. The entries below preserve each case separately and link to its original publication.   Selected Flagship Work  Shelf Analysis AI platform for Retail Compliance  For an unnamed retail technology SaaS provider, product detection and shelf-position analysis address slow manual compliance audits across distributed stores. The source reports 90%+ detection accuracy and 80% less audit time within the first month across 500+ locations; the provider serves 600+ customers. These are source-reported figures; the source does not provide the accuracy definition or baseline audit hours. Client Work  Document AI and automation  **Automating Customs Documentation with Local AI and Computer Vision.** An unnamed logistics and customs brokerage firm needed to reduce manual transcription of invoices, packing lists and IDs while keeping data local. Locally hosted OCR, computer vision and LLM extraction populate editable declaration templates. The case estimates up to 90% processing-time reduction and reports 97% fewer human errors versus manual entry; savings remain estimated, and MVP classification is user-selected, with automated classification planned. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/customs-document-automation) · PDF pp. 1–3.   **AI-powered Customs Intelligence System.** Fragmented HS-code definitions and historical declarations motivated a LangChain/GPT chatbot combining official definitions, semantic search and historical trade descriptions. Reported results are 76.5% Top-1 and 94.1% Top-3 HS-code accuracy, with MRR 0.8515; the stated corpus contains 65.5 million descriptions. Evaluation-set size and independent validation are not supplied. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/ai-customs-intelligence) · PDF pp. 1–4.  **Data Cleaning & Ingestion for HS Code Systems.** Scattered HS-code PDFs and duplicated trade descriptions obstructed retrieval. Official structures were normalized into Excel/MongoDB and descriptions deduplicated for Pinecone semantic search; about 65.5 million descriptions became about 2 million unique records, reported as a 97% reduction with frequency information retained. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/data-cleaning-hs-codes) · PDF pp. 1–3.  **Excel-based Automated Testing: Reducing Manual Validation Time.** Customs QA teams needed to validate large volumes of product descriptions more efficiently. An Excel-based FastAPI framework compares predicted and expected HS codes; its first run processed 11,000 descriptions in about four hours versus an estimated manual effort of more than a month. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/excel-automated-testing) · PDF pp. 1–4.  **LLM Results Analyzer: Unified Analysis Framework.** Customs classification users faced separate theoretical and historical systems and had to reconcile answers manually. Parallel Custom Clarification and Custom Optimization calls feed an analyzer LLM that preserves original outputs; the source reports unified, explained recommendations, without a quantified quality gain. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/llm-results-analyzer) · PDF pp. 1–4.  Computer vision  **A Global Beverage Leader Prevents Retail Fraud with AI.** An unnamed retail distribution technology provider addressed false or reused field-agent compliance evidence by integrating AI fraud detection with field-report and planogram workflows. The source reports over 90% fraudulent-submission detection accuracy in the first week and more than 90% improvement in audit reliability; the denominator and sample are not given, and client attribution needs confirmation. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/fraud-detection) · PDF pp. 1–4.   **Flexible Campaigns with Poster Recognition AI.** A retail technology/distribution provider sought to avoid repeated model training for each poster campaign. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/poster-recognition) · PDF pp. 1–3.  **Boosting Recognition Accuracy with AI-Powered Data Refinement.** For an unnamed workplace attendance/security organization, unusable face images and fragmented identities degraded attendance records. Image filtering, track-level identity aggregation, a virtual entry line and automatic face alignment reportedly improved data cleanliness and reduced misrecognition; the cited 5,000 unusable images per day describes the prior problem, not a measured reduction. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/improving-recognition-accuracy) · PDF pp. 1–3.   **IV Drip Flow Rate Estimator.** A medical technology provider sought to reduce the attention and observer variation involved in manual IV-drop counting. An offline iOS/Android camera estimator reportedly exceeds 95% accuracy and returns results within 15 seconds versus up to three minutes manually; the source describes this as over 10× faster. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/iv-drip-flow-rate-estimator) · PDF pp. 1–3.  **Field Center Detection for Augmented Reality in Sports.** An unnamed AR company needed to reduce manual field alignment. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/field-center-detection) · PDF pp. 1–3.  **Predicting Carcass Quality Before Slaughter with On-Device Computer Vision.** Cattle producers and feedlot managers need quality signals before post-slaughter grading. A unified mobile detector/classifier predicts premium, medium and poor bands from live-cattle images; the source reports 95% “precision accuracy” and offline inference. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/beef-carcass-detection) · PDF pp. 1–3.  **LiveScore AI Revolutionizes Real-Time Basketball Scoring.** Event organizers and venues needed faster scoring and simpler live operations. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/livescore-ai) · PDF pp. 1–4.  **Breast Cancer Biomarker Classification Revolutionizes Pathology Workflows.** Pathology workflows face costly, labor-intensive IHC testing. AI platforms  **Enterprise Data Labeling Platform Deployment & Customization.** A computer-vision service provider needed isolated annotation environments, backup and scalable model-assisted workflows. A customized labeling platform with an ML backend and GCS-backed recovery reportedly supports 15+ isolated deployments, 75% less annotation time and 90%+ labeling accuracy. The source’s 99.9% data-protection reliability claim is omitted pending a defined measure.   [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/data-labeling-tool) · PDF pp. 1–4.  **ROI Processing with Track-Timing Cuts LumioHub AI Costs.** Whole-frame processing and long-lived tracks strained compute and memory. [Original case](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/optimizing-recognition-processing) · PDF pp. 1–3.   **Securing a Scalable Platform.** An unnamed enterprise LumioHub AI client needed secure tenancy, permissions and scalable inference.
