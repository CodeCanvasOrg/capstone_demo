**Customs and international trade**

**Problem.** Scattered HS-code PDFs and duplicated trade descriptions impede retrieval.

**Reported outcome.** About 65.5 million descriptions reduced to about 2 million unique records, reported as 97% reduction, while retaining frequency information.

[Original case study](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/data-cleaning-hs-codes) · PDF `case-studies/data-cleaning-hs-codes.pdf`, pp. 1–3.

## Research

The following work appears in HumbleBeeAI’s research collection. Authors and findings remain attached to their individual papers. The supplied sources do not establish that these papers underpin any named product or client case. LITE and PRIME explicitly acknowledge HUMBLEBEE R&D support, and PRIME discusses LITE.

### LITE: A Paradigm Shift in Multi-Object Tracking with Efficient ReID Feature Integration

**Authors:** Jumabek Alikhanov, Dilshod Obidov, Hakil Kim.

**Status:** arXiv preprint v2, 1 October 2024.

**Question.** Can ReID features be integrated into detection/tracking without separate model overhead?

**Finding.** DeepSORT+LITE reports HOTA 43.03 at 28.3 FPS on MOT17, about twice DeepSORT speed on MOT17 and four times on MOT20 with similar accuracy. MOT17/MOT20 comparisons; framework, detector and configuration determine results.

[Publication / research record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04187) · PDF `papers/2409.04187-lite-the-lightweight-integrated-tracking-feature-extraction-paradi.pdf`, p. 1.

### Practical Evaluation Framework for Real-Time Multi-Object Tracking: Achieving Optimal and Realistic Performance

**Authors:** Jumabek Alikhanov, Dilshod Obidov, Mirsaid Abdurasulov, Hakil Kim.

**Status:** Published, IEEE Access, volume 13, 2025; DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3541177.

**Question.** How should tracking be evaluated under practical detector, ReID and runtime constraints?

**Finding.** Framework reports that resolution/threshold choices can yield HOTA up to 30 versus 10 without optimization; LITE reduces appearance-feature overhead. Configuration-dependent experimental finding, not a universal 20-point improvement.

[Publication / research record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10883969) · PDF `papers/10883969-prime-practical-evaluation-framework-for-real-time-multi-object-tr.pdf`, p. 1.

### Design of Contextual Filtered Features for Better Smartphone-User Receptivity Prediction

**Authors:** Jumabek Alikhanov, Panyu Zhang, Youngtae Noh; Hakil Kim.

**Status:** Published, IEEE Internet of Things Journal 11(7), 1 April 2024; listing says 2023.

**Question.** Do contextually filtered phone/watch features better predict receptivity to just-in-time interventions?

**Finding.** Average ROC-AUC 56.5% versus 54.7% for conventional features on KEmoPhone: 3,334 responses from 73 participants over one week. Across multiple classifiers; baseline conventional time-series statistics uses an 80-minute window.

[Publication / research record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10316180) · PDF `papers/10316180-design-of-contextual-filtered-features-for-better-smartphone-user-.pdf`, p. 1.

### Performance Evaluation of Deep Learning Based Network Intrusion Detection System across Multiple Balanced and Imbalanced Datasets

**Authors:** Azizjon Meliboev; Jumabek Alikhanov; Wooseong Kim.

**Status:** Published, Electronics 11(4), 515, 9 February 2022; DOI 10.3390/electronics11040515.

**Question.** How do CNN, recurrent and hybrid intrusion detectors compare across balanced/imbalanced benchmark data?

**Finding.** Single CNN and CNN/LSTM combinations outperform other evaluated architectures in the reported experiments. UNSW-NB15, KDDCup 99 and NSL-KDD; up to 100 epochs, learning rate 0.0001. Results vary by dataset and balance.

[Publication / research record](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/11/4/515) · PDF `papers/515-performance-evaluation-of-deep-learning-based-network-intrusion-de.pdf`, p. 1.

### Investigating the Effect of Traffic Sampling on Machine Learning-Based Network Intrusion Detection Approaches

**Authors:** Jumabek Alikhanov, Rhongho Jang, Mohammed Abuhamad, David Mohaisen, Daehun Nyang, Youngtae Noh.

**Status:** Published online 23 December 2021; IEEE Access volume 10, 2022; DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3137318.

**Question.** How do packet sampling choices affect intrusion-detection rate and false alarms?

**Finding.** Short malicious flows can disappear even at sampling rates 1/10 and 1/100; random forest with SketchFlow gives a favorable detection/false-alarm combination in the evaluated settings. Four samplers, three classifiers and rates 1/10, 1/100 and 1/1000; do not generalize to all networks.

[Publication / research record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9661375) · PDF `papers/9661375-investigating-the-effect-of-traffic-sampling-on-machine-learning-b.pdf`, p. 1.

### 1D CNN based network intrusion detection with normalization on imbalanced data

**Authors:** Azizjon Meliboev; Jumabek Alikhanov; Wooseong Kim.

**Status:** Downloaded evidence: arXiv:2003.00476v1, 1 March 2020; listing links an IEEE conference record.

**Question.** Can normalized 1D-CNN traffic models improve intrusion detection on imbalanced data?

**Finding.** 1D-CNN variants outperform RF/SVM comparators in the reported UNSW-NB15 experiments. Balanced and imbalanced training; up to 200 epochs, learning rate 0.0001; no single cross-setting accuracy claimed.

[Publication / research record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9064976) · PDF `papers/9064976-1d-cnn-based-network-intrusion-detection-with-normalization-on-imb.pdf`, p. 1.

### W-Net: A CNN-based Architecture for White Blood Cells Image Classification

**Authors:** Changhun Jung, Mohammed Abuhamad, Jumabek Alikhanov, Aziz Mohaisen, Kyungja Han, DaeHun Nyang.

**Status:** Downloaded evidence: arXiv:1910.01091v1, 2 October 2019.

**Question.** Can a compact CNN classify five white-blood-cell types?

**Finding.** Reports average 97% classification accuracy on a private 6,562-image dataset; also evaluates LISC transfer learning. Five WBC types; private Catholic University of Korea dataset. This is not a breast-cancer biomarker result.

[Publication / research record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.01091) · PDF `papers/1910.01091-w-net-a-cnn-based-architecture-for-white-blood-cells-image-classif.pdf`, p. 1.
