# Driving Brand Visibility on Generative AI with Amazon QuickSight

HumbleBeeAI built an Amazon QuickSight dashboard to help enterprise marketing, SEO and product teams measure how their brand appears across generative AI platforms. [PDF p. 1](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/05daf0de-82a8-408d-9cdc-b37ed15e2821/asset)

## Executive Summary

A leading enterprise client lacked a consistent way to understand its brand visibility in generative AI platforms such as ChatGPT and Gemini. HumbleBeeAI developed the Generative Engine Visibility Dashboard on Amazon QuickSight to centralize automated analytics and turn scattered generative-engine outputs into insights for marketing, SEO and product teams. The case study describes the dashboard as deployed; the client is unnamed. [PDF p. 1](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/a39bd092-c7a9-44a5-8776-f8e3ce7c728f/asset) [PDF p. 3](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/ec4865fe-0d2c-43c2-bbb0-fb31f838b6ec/asset)

The project is presented as a client case study, not an independent research study. No research authors, peer-review status or separate publication status are provided. [Read the complete HumbleBeeAI case study.](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/amplifyr)

## Context and Challenge

Before the dashboard, the client did not have a systematic way to track brand mentions, ranking positions or the context in which the brand appeared in generative AI responses. The case study characterizes the previous process as manual guesswork, making it difficult to use consistent evidence when making strategic decisions. [PDF p. 2](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/8fb19394-dd92-4d93-b11e-049c9676d26e/asset) [PDF p. 1](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/a39bd092-c7a9-44a5-8776-f8e3ce7c728f/asset)

The challenge extended beyond measurement. Generative engines produce large and growing volumes of unstructured text, which the client needed to convert into useful analytics. At the same time, marketing, SEO and product users needed to explore the information without depending on technical specialists for every question. [PDF p. 2](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/8fb19394-dd92-4d93-b11e-049c9676d26e/asset)

## Solution and Capabilities

HumbleBeeAI delivered a Generative Engine Visibility Dashboard centered on Amazon QuickSight. The reported capabilities address data collection, analysis and access for business users: [PDF p. 2](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/d3679728-8c8b-4152-81e5-9b006db0f57a/asset)

- Automated ingestion and processing: Amazon Athena queries data from sources including files in Amazon S3; Python scripts transform raw, unstructured generative-engine text into structured data. This is intended to reduce manual data handling and make the inputs usable for analysis. [PDF p. 2](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/d3679728-8c8b-4152-81e5-9b006db0f57a/asset)
- Visibility metrics: QuickSight calculated fields and Level-Aware Calculations (LAC) are used to present brand-mention frequency, ranking positions, visibility trends and comparisons with competitors. [PDF p. 2](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/d3679728-8c8b-4152-81e5-9b006db0f57a/asset)
- Interactive exploration: A multi-tab dashboard with charts, graphs and tables supports filtering and drill-down by companies, date ranges and other variables, so users can inspect performance at different levels of detail. [PDF p. 2](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/d3679728-8c8b-4152-81e5-9b006db0f57a/asset)
- AWS and QuickSight SPICE: The case study describes the architecture as designed to support large and growing data volumes. It does not provide a quantified capacity or performance benchmark. [PDF p. 3](http://localhost:3344/workspaces/ab6b13a5-514c-4849-96d2-cd4008c5c310/documents/bce2fae1-0935-468e-864f-dd8e81736753/evidence/26500ec6-600f-4323-99c9-f354f0ca9ac1/asset)
