# Field Center Detection for Augmented Reality in Sports

Field Center Detection for Augmented Reality in Sports — a mobile computer-vision application designed to locate sports-field centers and alignment points for augmented reality. [PDF source: bea4afff-bd56-4e26-95d5-6057b5e68e8e]

## Executive Summary

HumbleBeeAI’s case study describes work with an unnamed augmented reality company seeking to reduce the friction of aligning a sports field through a phone. The described solution uses offline mobile segmentation and keypoint estimation to identify the field center and alignment points. The source reports real-time localization on iOS and Android. The case-study record is a review draft: owner approval is pending, and the materials do not establish whether the application is currently available in production. ## Context and Challenge

The project addresses a practical constraint for sports broadcasting, coaching and AR experiences: precise overlays depend on reliable field alignment, while manual measurements and physical markers can take time and be prone to error. The case study also identifies cost and real-time feedback as challenges for approaches relying on specialized cameras or sensors. In a dynamic sports setting, inaccurate or slow alignment can disrupt the intended AR experience. ## Solution and Capabilities

### Offline field localization

The described mobile application combines lightweight segmentation models with keypoint estimation. A user points a phone camera at the field; the system identifies its center and key alignment points, and the source says it works without an internet connection on iOS and Android. [PDF source: 3ddd653f-c2cf-4fcb-b625-38dff4e90e6d]

### Mobile-oriented workflow
