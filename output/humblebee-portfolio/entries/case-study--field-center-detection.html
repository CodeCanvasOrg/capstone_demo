Field Center Detection for Augmented Reality in Sports

Field Center Detection for Augmented Reality in Sports — a mobile computer-vision application designed to locate sports-field centers and alignment points for augmented reality. [PDF p. 1]

Executive Summary

HumbleBeeAI’s case study describes work with an unnamed augmented reality company seeking to reduce the friction of aligning a sports field through a phone. The described solution uses offline mobile segmentation and keypoint estimation to identify the field center and alignment points. The source reports real-time localization on iOS and Android. The case-study record is a review draft: owner approval is pending, and the materials do not establish whether the application is currently available in production. Original case-study page [PDF p. 1] [PDF p. 2]

Context and Challenge

The project addresses a practical constraint for sports broadcasting, coaching and AR experiences: precise overlays depend on reliable field alignment, while manual measurements and physical markers can take time and be prone to error. The case study also identifies cost and real-time feedback as challenges for approaches relying on specialized cameras or sensors. In a dynamic sports setting, inaccurate or slow alignment can disrupt the intended AR experience. [PDF p. 2] [PDF p. 2]

Solution and Capabilities

Offline field localization

The described mobile application combines lightweight segmentation models with keypoint estimation. A user points a phone camera at the field; the system identifies its center and key alignment points, and the source says it works without an internet connection on iOS and Android. [PDF p. 2]

Mobile-oriented workflow

The case-study material also describes customizable thresholds, exportable logs and integration with existing mobile applications. These are features stated in the source; the available record does not provide further implementation details or establish their deployment status independently. [PDF p. 2]
