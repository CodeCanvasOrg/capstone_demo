# Breast Cancer Biomarker Classification Revolutionizes Pathology Workflows

HumbleBeeAI developed an H&E-image biomarker triage tool for pathology laboratories that predicts four breast cancer biomarkers and is described as ready for clinical validation. [PDF source: 5e637708-d89f-4830-a0e4-69a9437a07ad]

## Executive Summary

The Breast Cancer Biomarker Classification project serves pathology laboratories seeking to manage the cost and labor involved in immunohistochemistry (IHC) biomarker workflows. Deep-learning models trained on the IHC4BC dataset predict ER, PR, Ki-67 and HER2 from routine H&E histology images. The case study describes a web application for triage, but states that clinical validation is still needed; it supplies no numerical diagnostic-performance result. [PDF source: 517384ad-a5d7-4fd1-bdea-19496c92b907] [PDF source: 08c58391-a495-4209-928e-8174e32d3468] [PDF source: 5e637708-d89f-4830-a0e4-69a9437a07ad]

## Context and Challenge

Traditional testing uses IHC panels to assess these biomarkers. The source describes this workflow as time-consuming, costly and labor-intensive, with manual evaluation requiring expertise and presenting scaling challenges in high-volume settings. It also identifies pathology backlogs and variability in manual assessments as concerns. These are the stated workflow challenges; the reviewed materials do not report a measured baseline, test conditions or quantified delays. [PDF source: 517384ad-a5d7-4fd1-bdea-19496c92b907] [PDF source: 9dd75fc2-6f3e-4bfc-9759-90d65868d470]

## Solution and Capabilities

The solution applies deep-learning models trained on IHC4BC to routine H&E images, producing predictions for ER, PR, Ki-67 and HER2. It is intended to help prioritize cases for further testing, rather than establish a replacement for confirmatory IHC. [PDF source: 08c58391-a495-4209-928e-8174e32d3468] [PDF source: 2bfc3061-1ddf-466a-bdbb-ecba90749eb2]

### Web application for triage

The case study describes a web app that accepts slide uploads, supports batch inference, displays positive/negative examples and makes results exportable for downstream reporting. The source presents integration with laboratory information systems (LIS) or laboratory management systems (LIMS) as an intended integration capability, not as a confirmed production deployment. [PDF source: bed20b65-6294-4738-b677-717f26cda181]

## Results and Business Impact

The supported outcome is a described web triage tool, positioned to support clinical validation. Although the source makes qualitative claims about speed, consistency and cost savings, no numerical diagnostic-performance result or measured cost-saving study is provided. Its reference to per-case reagent costs falling from “hundreds of dollars” to “a fraction” supplies no exact amount, comparison conditions or measured study, so it should not be treated as an established business result. ## Limitations and Next Steps

Clinical validation is pending. The reviewed material does not provide validation protocols, numerical performance results, diagnostic approval, measured savings or evidence that the tool replaces confirmatory testing. Clinical workflow integration is described as a future application, not a verified deployment. No additional roadmap or next steps have been provided. ## Conclusion

The project demonstrates a web-based approach to using H&E images to triage breast cancer biomarker testing across four markers. Its value is currently described as workflow support; clinical validation and measured diagnostic or financial outcomes remain unreported.
