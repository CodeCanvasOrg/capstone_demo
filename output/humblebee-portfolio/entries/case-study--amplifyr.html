Driving Brand Visibility on Generative AI

A HumbleBeeAI dashboard built with Amazon QuickSight helps an enterprise client measure its brand visibility across generative AI platforms. [PDF p. 1]

Executive Summary

HumbleBeeAI developed the Generative Engine Visibility Dashboard for an unnamed enterprise client whose marketing, SEO and product teams needed a consistent way to understand how the brand appeared on platforms such as ChatGPT and Gemini. The project addresses visibility measurement through centralized analytics; the source describes the dashboard as delivered. The reviewed case-study PDF is a website snapshot, and owner approval is pending. View the original case study. [PDF p. 1]

Context and Challenge

The client lacked a systematic method for tracking brand mentions, rankings and the context of appearances in generative AI responses. The prior process is described as manual and difficult to scale: outputs could be large and unstructured, making analysis time-consuming and prone to error. Marketing, SEO and product users also needed a way to explore the information without depending on technical specialists for every question. [PDF p. 2] [PDF p. 2]

Solution and Capabilities

HumbleBeeAI built a centralized analytics dashboard using Amazon QuickSight to turn generative-engine data into structured visibility measures. [PDF p. 2]

Automated data processing and visibility measures

The source describes Amazon Athena querying data from multiple sources, including files in Amazon S3, with Python scripts used for more complex transformations of unstructured text. QuickSight calculated fields and Level-Aware Calculations support measures such as mention frequency, ranking positions and comparisons with competitors. This processing is intended to reduce manual data handling and make brand performance easier to analyze. [PDF p. 2]

Interactive exploration

The dashboard is described as a multi-tabbed interface with charts, tables, filters and drill-downs by company and date range. These controls let marketing, SEO and product teams examine the reported data from different angles and access insights directly. [PDF p. 2]

Results and Business Impact

The case study reports that the dashboard replaced a manual process with automated reporting and gave teams a clearer, data-backed view for analysis and decision-making. It does not provide a quantified brand lift, a before-and-after business comparison, or independent validation of business impact. A 100% automation cover label has no defined denominator or validation conditions, so it is not presented here as a measured result. [PDF p. 3] [PDF p. 1]

Limitations and Next Steps

The source material does not specify validation methods, test conditions, or measured accuracy, and the unnamed client prevents further context about deployment scope. The case study describes future work as focusing on adding data sources and refining analytical models; these are stated as ongoing partnership priorities, not capabilities confirmed as delivered. Owner approval of the website-snapshot PDF remains pending. [PDF p. 3] [PDF p. 1]

Conclusion

The Generative Engine Visibility Dashboard gives an enterprise client's marketing, SEO and product teams a shared way to inspect brand visibility in generative AI platforms. Its supported value is centralized analytics and easier exploration; quantified visibility gains or business outcomes have not been provided. [PDF p. 1] [PDF p. 3]
