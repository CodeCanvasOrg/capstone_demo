# 1D CNN based network intrusion detection with normalization on imbalanced data

A research study evaluates normalized 1D-CNN models for network intrusion detection on imbalanced UNSW-NB15 traffic. [PDF source: 8eff2167-12de-4208-8bdf-43cf92c8a556]

## Executive Summary

“1D CNN based network intrusion detection with normalization on imbalanced data” by Azizjon Meliboev, Jumabek Alikhanov and Wooseong Kim asks whether normalized 1D-CNN traffic models can improve detection on imbalanced data. This is research, not a reported HumbleBeeAI client deployment or product. ## Context and Challenge

Signature-based detection can miss unknown malicious behavior, while anomaly detection seeks deviations from normal traffic. The study investigates supervised learning for classifying normal versus attack traffic, with class imbalance as a specific constraint. It uses UNSW-NB15 and compares CNN models with Random Forest (RF) and Support Vector Machine (SVM) classifiers. [PDF source: 6ea83cd5-bfe9-47fb-b5a5-cbed05d1de56] [PDF source: 8eff2167-12de-4208-8bdf-43cf92c8a556]

## Technical Approach

The paper describes serializing TCP/IP packets within a predetermined time range and labeling traffic as normal or abnormal for supervised classification. Its models use 1D convolutions to learn traffic features, with batch normalization and softmax classification; experiments also include a CNN–LSTM combination. The best reported CNN configuration has three convolutional layers, two max-pooling layers, batch normalization, dropout and two fully connected layers, taking input of shape 42×1. [PDF source: 8eff2167-12de-4208-8bdf-43cf92c8a556] [PDF source: 7ef7dadd-e4c5-4de7-ba67-be302ae7e336]

## Implementation

The study reports experiments on balanced and imbalanced data, using random oversampling to balance normal and attack records. Models were run for up to 200 epochs at a learning rate of 0.0001; batch size was 32 for the networks except the CNN–LSTM combination, which used 64. Implementation and GPU CUDA acceleration are described in the paper. The evidence establishes an experimental model, not a deployed monitoring integration or production service. [PDF source: f5212d51-2a5c-484e-bdfb-20dc180135b9] [PDF source: 7ef7dadd-e4c5-4de7-ba67-be302ae7e336]

## Evaluation

The study compares normalized 1D-CNN models with Random Forest and SVM on balanced and imbalanced UNSW-NB15 binary-classification data. It evaluates accuracy, precision, recall and F-score, with network training for up to 200 epochs and learning rate 0.0001. The paper reports benefits from the normalized CNN approach, while model rankings depend on the balance condition and metric. These benchmark comparisons do not establish live-network performance or business impact.

## Results and Business Impact

Technically, the results show stronger reported CNN performance on several balanced-data metrics and high recall for the three-layer model on imbalanced data, while RF has higher imbalanced-data accuracy and precision. The paper reports no client, operational deployment, financial outcome or measured business impact; these experimental metrics should not be interpreted as business results.
