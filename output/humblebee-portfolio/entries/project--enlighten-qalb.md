# Enlighten Qalb: AI Spiritual Guide

Enlighten Qalb is a memory-enabled AI companion for people seeking faith-aligned spiritual reflection and guidance. [PDF source: 7615dc48-baea-49b7-9955-d9553cf76261]

## Executive Summary

Enlighten Qalb is described as an in-use product, and the reviewed project record labels it “prod ready.” It is intended to support ongoing emotional and spiritual reflection through a conversational companion grounded in curated spiritual material and personalized memory. The source does not provide a demo URL, deployment details, or measured user outcomes. See the [HumbleBeeAI project page](https://humblebee.ai/projects/enlighten-qalb). [PDF source: 7615dc48-baea-49b7-9955-d9553cf76261] [PDF source: 7c9f3beb-7e58-41dc-b0cd-a38061d2eb99] [PDF source: 37336d44-f180-4ad9-aa1c-062172198b57]

## User Need

The product is for people seeking spiritual reflection in a faith-aligned context. Its brief identifies a common user difficulty: digital assistants can offer generic, isolated advice, and users may have to repeat their story, goals, and concerns in each conversation. The project also recognizes that emotional reflections are personal and that users may be uncomfortable storing them in opaque systems. [PDF source: 7c9f3beb-7e58-41dc-b0cd-a38061d2eb99] [PDF source: b6915366-1dbd-460b-aa1e-359892fc7f35] [PDF source: f4dc8cdf-a27b-473f-92b2-33920b705adf]

## Product Vision

The product direction is to make reflection more continuous and personally relevant, bringing curated spiritual wisdom together with memory of earlier conversations. Its stated longer-term vision includes supporting inner clarity, emotional balance, and connection with Allah. These are intended benefits, not independently measured results; the available source material does not report outcome testing. [PDF source: 12c99c5d-6989-4553-b41b-81ddd57a2eff] [PDF source: 7615dc48-baea-49b7-9955-d9553cf76261]

## Capabilities and Experience

The documented experience centers on a conversational guide initially grounded in The Alchemy of Happiness (Kimyai Saadat) by Al-Ghazali. The product describes structured, searchable source material and relevant passage retrieval to ground responses. Its memory system is described as retaining preferences, reflections, emotional themes, goals, and communication style so that conversations can build on previous context. [PDF source: 37336d44-f180-4ad9-aa1c-062172198b57] [PDF source: 7b6e126c-158b-40f2-9a20-86e97e4b0831] [PDF source: df666180-0cb7-4826-9b37-fa8badd312df]

The overview identifies two guidance modes, Ustath and Enthusiast; Enthusiast is described as using simpler language and less terminology for users newer to spiritual concepts. The product materials also describe privacy-oriented design, including secure conversation storage, a local-first approach where possible, and controlled cloud sync. These are descriptions in the project material, not independently verified security findings. Monthly emotional summaries, mood and stress trends, reflection tracking, and growth indicators are explicitly marked “Coming Soon.” [PDF source: 7615dc48-baea-49b7-9955-d9553cf76261] [PDF source: 12c99c5d-6989-4553-b41b-81ddd57a2eff] [PDF source: 66fdefd3-2d47-4f3f-af8c-57b9ea44f452] [PDF source: d54b015a-2218-4a56-9bc1-5b37a23b698a]

## Differentiators

The supported distinction is its combination of faith-aligned source material, persistent personal context, and adjustable guidance style within one spiritual-reflection companion. The sources describe an initial focus on Al-Ghazali’s work and ongoing memory; they do not provide comparative testing against other products or evidence of superior outcomes.
