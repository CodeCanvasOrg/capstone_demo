# Wakil AI

Wakil AI combines a multilingual, evidence-based legal assistant with a lawyer marketplace to help people and organizations navigate legal services in Uzbekistan.  [PDF source: 31142cf4-8e1c-430c-9b24-8b797ce16319] [PDF source: 31142cf4-8e1c-430c-9b24-8b797ce16319]

## Executive Summary

Wakil AI is a legal technology product for individuals, businesses and legal professionals in Uzbekistan. The reviewed project record lists its published status as “prod ready”; users can visit the product at [https://wakil.ai/](https://wakil.ai/). Its stated purpose is to make legal information easier to understand and connect users with professional legal support. The source material describes the platform’s purpose and capabilities but does not provide measured user or business outcomes.  [PDF source: 31142cf4-8e1c-430c-9b24-8b797ce16319] [PDF source: f670158d-0e5c-47d1-ab21-1285e09c430a]

## User Need

Citizens and enterprises may encounter complex legal texts, limited plain-language explanations in official databases, and difficulty finding or affording professional help. Wakil AI is intended to address those access barriers for people who need understandable legal information, as well as lawyers seeking a way to reach potential clients.  [PDF source: f670158d-0e5c-47d1-ab21-1285e09c430a] [PDF source: bf81721f-0ca2-44c2-8152-838cb9b23b77]

## Product Vision

The product direction brings AI-supported legal information together with human legal services, with local language support and grounding in Uzbekistan’s legal sources. A private AI companion for casework, Wakil AI Associate, is described as upcoming; the source does not establish it as generally available. The materials do not specify further approved roadmap commitments.  [PDF source: 31142cf4-8e1c-430c-9b24-8b797ce16319]

## Capabilities and Experience

The legal assistant is described as using retrieval-augmented generation to ground answers in authoritative legal sources and provide links to relevant legal provisions. Published language coverage is Uzbek (Latin and Cyrillic), Russian and English. The described search approach routes specific law-number questions to deterministic lookup rather than standard vector search. The marketplace is intended to connect users with verified legal professionals for personalized advice, consultation and advocacy.  [PDF source: 07030454-7e84-450a-a073-911010149535] [PDF source: 897e3b5f-2cdd-4551-9583-f93db794436c] [PDF source: bf81721f-0ca2-44c2-8152-838cb9b23b77]

Associate is described in the PDF snapshot as a private casework companion with case-specific workspace, context retention and professional-support integration; another passage says it is rolling out through limited early-access invitations. Its exact availability is therefore not clear from the material, and it should not be treated as a generally released feature.  [PDF source: 308fa032-fa3e-4513-ad45-18b0c72659d3]

## Differentiators

Wakil AI’s stated distinguishing focus is the combination of multilingual legal assistance, source-linked answers and access to lawyers in one product. The PDF reports an evaluation on 170+ real-world Soliq (Tax Law) queries using the RAGAS evaluation framework. It provides no score, comparison basis, test conditions or result values, so this evaluation scope is not evidence of a quantified advantage or client outcome.
