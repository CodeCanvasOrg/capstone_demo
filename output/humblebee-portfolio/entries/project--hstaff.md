# HStaff

HStaff brings Hubstaff activity, Discord progress reports and team analytics together for leaders of distributed teams. [PDF source: efe49f50-b2ac-4bdf-aa35-5d285b6c1e34] Project page: [HStaff](https://humblebee.ai/projects/hstaff).

## Executive Summary

HStaff is presented as a workforce analytics platform that consolidates work activity and team progress information in a shared dashboard. The reviewed project record lists its published status as “prod ready.” No demo URL is provided in the record, and production access or deployment details have not been supplied.

## User Need

Distributed-team leaders need a clear view of work and reporting across separate systems. The project material describes Hubstaff as the source of work-hour and productivity data, Discord as the home for daily progress reports, and internal systems as a source of team relationships. It identifies the resulting fragmentation, manual collection and calculation, and lack of a central way to monitor report submissions as management burdens. [PDF source: 97b23065-e904-420c-8ae0-352178bce73b] [PDF source: efe49f50-b2ac-4bdf-aa35-5d285b6c1e34]

## Product Vision

The product direction is a unified, accessible view that helps managers review activity, progress and team structure without manually assembling information from separate platforms. The project materials describe mobile access as part of the intended experience; no measured change in management efficiency or administrative effort is reported. [PDF source: bb22044f-3165-4a84-84a9-ce79b66bf59e]

## Capabilities and Experience

The documented capabilities include Hubstaff activity collection with hourly synchronization and historical data for trend analysis, alongside synchronization of daily Discord progress reports. A dashboard summarizes tracked hours, deepwork measures, recent activity and progress-planning compliance. Reports provide individual and project views with date-range selection; user detail views show activity by day, project time allocation, deepwork and tracked hours. The materials also describe mentorship and team-relationship tracking, filtering, and responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile. [PDF source: 512e5df4-2fdc-4ba3-b65f-174e2af64b09] [PDF source: 276911a9-f002-4b38-b12b-00afa0468eae] [PDF source: 9610a7c5-0ce9-42ff-a574-dfebd4522a96] [PDF source: d4f96de4-f9c5-4b93-97b9-1fa97130c0c6]

The source describes synchronization as hourly for Hubstaff and “real-time” for new Discord reports, but supplies no test conditions or measured reliability figures. These are product descriptions, not independently reported performance results. [PDF source: 512e5df4-2fdc-4ba3-b65f-174e2af64b09] [PDF source: 276911a9-f002-4b38-b12b-00afa0468eae]

## Differentiators

HStaff’s documented distinction is its combination of data from Hubstaff, Discord and team relationships in one place, with built-in views for activity and progress compliance. The materials also describe automatic deepwork calculation and responsive layouts. No comparative testing or evidence supporting superiority over other products has been provided.
