# Shelf Analysis AI platform for Retail Compliance

## Executive Summary

HumbleBeeAI reports delivering Shelf Analysis AI for an unnamed retail technology SaaS provider whose distribution platform serves more than 600 customers across FMCG, beverage, pharmaceutical and automotive industries. The system was integrated into that platform and used to automate product detection and shelf-position analysis across 500+ retail locations. The case study reports 90%+ detection accuracy and 80% less manual audit time within the first month. The supplied PDF text has been reviewed, but owner approval and portfolio feedback are pending. ## Context and Challenge

The provider’s customers and field teams needed to check product placement and merchandising compliance across distributed store networks. The previous process relied on manual shelf audits: agents spent hours counting products and checking planogram compliance, which limited coverage and delayed identification of competitor products in exclusive brand zones. Audits also had to contend with varied lighting, camera angles, shelf configurations and packaging, complicating consistent analysis.
