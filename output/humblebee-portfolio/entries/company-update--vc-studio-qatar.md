# HumbleBeeAI Expands to Qatar with the Launch of Venture Studio LLC

HumbleBeeAI establishes a Qatar venture studio as a planned Middle East hub for building future AI ventures. [PDF source: 0ee540f1-02e0-4a88-a8ef-6692aaea0293]

## Executive Summary

HumbleBeeAI announced the establishment of HumbleBeeAI Venture Studio LLC in the Qatar Financial Centre (QFC), positioning the entity as a hub to support launching, incubating and scaling future AI-driven ventures in the Middle East. The company describes the studio as a foundation for bringing its engineering, product-development and AI talent ecosystem into the region. Operational setup and partnership-building are still described as forthcoming, not completed outcomes.
