# LiveScore AI Revolutionizes Real-Time Basketball Scoring

LiveScore AI: real-time scoring and event operations for basketball trick-shot and dunk competitions

## Executive Summary

LiveScore AI is a computer-vision scoring platform for basketball event organizers, producers and venues. It is intended to address delayed feedback and the administrative burden of manually scoring trick-shot and dunk events by bringing movement and trick recognition, scores, leaderboards and event controls into one system. The reviewed case-study snapshot describes the platform and its capabilities; owner approval and portfolio feedback are still pending. ## Context and Challenge

In trick-shot and dunk competitions, organizers need to communicate outcomes as events unfold. The source describes manual scoring as prone to delays and human error, while score adjustments and leaderboard maintenance can interrupt event operations. It also identifies challenges keeping players and spectators connected to scoring and managing multiple concurrent games as event demand grows. No event sample, prior-workflow measurements or test conditions are provided, so the scale of these operational problems is not quantified. ## Solution and Capabilities

### Automated scoring and video processing

The described system processes live video on-site using NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano devices and supports RTSP streams from networked CCTV cameras as well as cameras connected directly to the device. Its scoring engine is described as recognizing player movement, tricks and dunk events, with the goal of reducing manual scoring. The source also says processed video is relayed to client displays alongside scoring data.  [PDF source: 59f8cb82-95fa-4a10-a7cf-e0ebb6b7d208] [PDF source: 6a1ec4ec-b0f7-4f0d-8df4-979d508a308e]

### Event displays and administration

Animated scoring displays and live trick queues are intended to make results visible to spectators and players. Persistent leaderboards update during events and are described as available for performance review, rankings and export. Admin controls support score edits, leaderboard adjustments and event management without interrupting gameplay, according to the case study.
