# From Collaboration to Innovation: Inside the Innerworks and HumblebeeAI Partnership

HumbleBeeAI partnered with Innerworks from January 2024 as its R&D and product arm to build cybersecurity and behavioral-intelligence capabilities for digital platforms. [PDF source: f5476103-0580-4b18-98c7-ed2fb9e856ae]

## Executive Summary

Innerworks serves exchanges, finance platforms, and digital ecosystems seeking to identify and block bad actors. The company update describes a collaboration that began in January 2024, with HumbleBeeAI supporting research and product development rather than providing only outsourced delivery. Its published account says the work covered foundational infrastructure and capabilities; owner approval of the reviewed source remains pending. [PDF source: f5476103-0580-4b18-98c7-ed2fb9e856ae] The original company update is available at [HumbleBeeAI’s Innerworks partnership page](https://humblebee.ai/case-studies/innerworks-partnership). [PDF source: 3b0bfd1c-bd4b-4159-a559-7ce6d27888b7]

## Context and Challenge

At the start of the engagement, Innerworks reportedly had a single SDK for foundational detection and fingerprinting, but lacked a fully integrated solution. The source says the team also lacked dedicated R&D capacity, senior engineering leadership in fraud and AI, and infrastructure to develop separate modules into a cohesive platform. This challenge was heightened by tightening exchange regulations and fraud methods involving generative AI and identity spoofing that were described as outpacing legacy detection systems. [PDF source: 5177d43d-341e-4ece-ad3e-1ee32decf6d8]

## Solution and Capabilities

Innerworks set product direction; HumbleBeeAI describes its role as researching, prototyping, iterating, and building capabilities alongside Innerworks’ teams. [PDF source: ddff9f68-cd6c-49ff-a941-b43a946e86a7]

### Device and behavioral intelligence

The reported scope includes device fingerprinting and behavioral profiling, within a solution stack for account-takeover protection and deeper behavioral analysis. The source describes more than 200 data points across device, network, and behavioral signals. This is a count of signals in the solution scope—not a detection-accuracy result. [PDF source: f5476103-0580-4b18-98c7-ed2fb9e856ae]

### VPN, bot, and adversarial defense

The update lists VPN and true-location detection, bot detection, threat intelligence, and a RedTeam adversarial-testing framework. It says components were structured modularly for R&D and to support production integration, with independent operation or combination in risk-scoring flows. It also reports iterative MVP launches, feedback collection, and model hardening against adversarial behavior. [PDF source: 80ae1796-b7e5-4a80-96cf-93f75f564baa]

The described engineering work included data-ingestion and feature-extraction pipelines, model serving, API endpoints, and monitoring. The source emphasizes knowledge transfer, with research, code, documentation, and design artifacts shared in Innerworks’ repositories. [PDF source: 80ae1796-b7e5-4a80-96cf-93f75f564baa] [PDF source: 4f9b8d7a-5b02-43fd-a402-c1c5d559e211]

## Results and Business Impact

The company update reports that Innerworks raised a $4M seed round in August 2025 led by AlbionVC. This is funding raised by Innerworks, not HumbleBeeAI; although the update associates the partnership’s infrastructure work with enabling the company’s development, the available evidence does not establish that the partnership alone caused the investment. [PDF source: f5476103-0580-4b18-98c7-ed2fb9e856ae] [PDF source: 4f9b8d7a-5b02-43fd-a402-c1c5d559e211]

The same update says Innerworks now uses its full suite across exchanges and digital platforms and maintains a stable client base. No detection accuracy, before-and-after comparison, client count, or business-impact measurement is provided; these reported observations should not be read as independently validated results. [PDF source: 4f9b8d7a-5b02-43fd-a402-c1c5d559e211]

## Limitations and Next Steps

The source distinguishes modular R&D capabilities from support for production integration; it does not provide test conditions, performance measurements, or independent validation for the detection modules. The reported availability of the full suite is a statement in the company update, not a detailed deployment record. No further roadmap or next-step plan is supplied. The source says knowledge, research, code, documentation, and design artifacts remain in Innerworks’ repositories to support handover and future scaling. [PDF source: 80ae1796-b7e5-4a80-96cf-93f75f564baa] [PDF source: 4f9b8d7a-5b02-43fd-a402-c1c5d559e211]

## Conclusion

The reported partnership combined R&D and product collaboration to expand Innerworks’ device, VPN, bot, and adversarial-defense capabilities, with modular architecture and repository-based knowledge transfer. The evidence supports describing the work and Innerworks’ reported funding milestone, but not attributing that investment solely to the partnership or claiming measured detection performance. [PDF source: f5476103-0580-4b18-98c7-ed2fb9e856ae] [PDF source: 4f9b8d7a-5b02-43fd-a402-c1c5d559e211]
