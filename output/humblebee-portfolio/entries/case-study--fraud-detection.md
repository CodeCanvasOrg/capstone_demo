# A Global Beverage Leader Prevents Retail Fraud with AI

## Executive Summary

This project addressed the risk of field agents submitting false or reused compliance evidence in retail audits. HumbleBeeAI integrated fraud detection into an unnamed retail technology provider’s cloud distribution platform, which serves more than 600 customers across industries including FMCG and beverage. ## Context and Challenge

Field teams manually audited product displays across thousands of retail locations. The source describes reports containing images of device screens, miniature reference displays and closed coolers, making audit evidence unreliable. Manual verification at this scale was labor-intensive and costly; inconsistent field data also constrained merchandising and retail-execution decisions. The affected platform users included distributors and manufacturers relying on field agents to verify in-store execution. [PDF source: 1de9ea14-99c9-4c41-986d-e14270819d32] [PDF source: 822425a5-8565-4599-a9ca-9f70c101ff79]

## Solution and Capabilities

### Integrated fraud checks

HumbleBeeAI delivered an AI fraud-detection system integrated with retail field-report and planogram workflows. Its described checks target three forms of suspect evidence: a classification model detects images captured from a phone or tablet screen; a separate model identifies closed cooler doors; and an OCR-based algorithm examines product-label text to identify miniature or printed reference images. Together, these checks support validation of submitted visual evidence within the distribution platform.
