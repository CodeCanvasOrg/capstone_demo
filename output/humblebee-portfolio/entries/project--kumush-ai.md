# Kumush AI

Kumush AI is a voice-agent product for organizations serving Uzbek-speaking customers, designed to handle calls, reservations and business workflows. [PDF source: c8f65be7-5ace-4d44-87c0-858f8096df0d]

## Executive Summary

Kumush AI addresses the need for voice interactions that work naturally in Uzbek, including regional dialects and local business processes. The portfolio record classifies the project as “prod demo”; although the PDF describes it as production-ready and deployed with early customers, that status has not been owner-confirmed. The demo is available at [kumush.ai](https://kumush.ai/). [PDF source: c8f65be7-5ace-4d44-87c0-858f8096df0d] [PDF source: be2f4dab-b536-40d9-b058-928b5f150d1b] [PDF source: 6349bf02-1fcb-4d61-b838-34c64f2a8873]

## User Need

Organizations serving Uzbek-speaking customers may need callers to get help in their language without being limited to translated scripts or basic speech recognition. The source describes needs including understanding dialects and colloquialisms, maintaining context across turns and supporting locally relevant workflows; it names education, healthcare, banking, hospitality and retail among intended sectors. [PDF source: c8f65be7-5ace-4d44-87c0-858f8096df0d] [PDF source: d4a66de6-1282-4a70-be3e-70d84950cc31]

## Product Vision

The stated direction is a configurable service that can support multiple organizations while adapting each agent’s voice, instructions, knowledge and tools to that organization. This is a description of the product approach in the PDF, not confirmation that every capability is available in the demo or in production. [PDF source: c2215075-b2dd-4247-a945-ad9325376c08] [PDF source: 1b697f09-3b38-40c5-a8ab-335bfca13c5b]

## Capabilities and Experience

The PDF describes inbound voice conversations, multi-turn context, Uzbek-language handling, reservations and workflow actions, connections to business knowledge and tools, and handoff to a person for complex cases. It also describes an administrative dashboard for managing agents and reviewing sessions. These are source-described capabilities; their specific deployment status is not established by the portfolio record. No images are included because figure permissions are pending. ## Differentiators

The documented focus is native Uzbek conversation, including dialects, code-switching and colloquial speech, alongside client-specific voice and instructions. These are stated product characteristics, not evidence of superiority over other platforms; comparative testing is not provided. ## Current Availability and Roadmap

Current listed availability is “prod demo,” with a public demo at [https://kumush.ai/](https://kumush.ai/); the project page is [https://humblebee.ai/projects/kumush-ai](https://humblebee.ai/projects/kumush-ai). The PDF reports 100% inbound call coverage in a production deployment, approximately 85% of calls resolved without human handoff, response times reduced from hours to seconds, satisfaction scores increased by roughly 40%, and support operating costs reduced by about 90%. These are reported claims, not verified client results here: deployment identity, sample sizes, measurement periods and test conditions are not supplied, and owner confirmation is pending. No roadmap or future plans have been provided. [PDF source: 6349bf02-1fcb-4d61-b838-34c64f2a8873] [PDF source: f8a39c71-bc3c-4c50-a15d-bd85c918e33a]
