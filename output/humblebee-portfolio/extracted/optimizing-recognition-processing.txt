 Executive Summary
 A leading LumioHub AI platform provider faced significant computational and memory challenges that
 hindered system performance and scalability. By partnering with HumbleBee AI, the client
 implemented a Region of Interest (ROI) processing model and a track-timing mechanism. This custom
 AI solution resulted in substantially lower computational costs, controlled memory usage, and more
 efficient, real-time data processing.

 Introduction
 The client operates at the forefront of workplace management technology, offering the LumioHub AI
 platform—a next-generation solution for modern enterprises. Their system leverages advanced
 Computer Vision and real-time analytics to automate attendance, bolster on-premise security, and
 streamline administrative workflows. With a cloud-native design and a focus on enterprise-grade
 privacy, the platform is engineered to help organizations modernize their operations while ensuring
 accuracy, compliance, and scalability. As a provider of cutting-edge AI-driven services, the client's
 platform must perform flawlessly under demanding conditions, processing high volumes of video data
 to deliver consistent and reliable insights.

 The Problem
 As the client expanded its deployment, their LumioHub AI platform encountered two critical
 performance bottlenecks that threatened its efficiency and scalability. Excessive Computational Load:
 The platform's Computer Vision models processed entire video frames, even when faces were only
 visible in small, specific sections. This comprehensive analysis of unnecessary data led to a significant
 increase in computational expenditure, slowing down processing times and straining system
 resources. RAM Overload from Prolonged Tracking: The system was designed to save every frame of
 an active track until the tracked individual disappeared from the camera's view. When a person
 remained visible for an extended period—sometimes over an hour—the sheer volume of saved frames
 created a severe memory bottleneck. This issue risked system instability and placed a hard limit on the
 platform's ability to handle high-traffic environments.

 The Solution
 HumbleBee AI was engaged to architect and implement a sophisticated, two-pronged solution
 designed to address the client's specific performance challenges. Our team of elite AI engineers
 developed a custom strategy focused on intelligent data processing and resource management.
 Region of Interest (ROI) Processing: To tackle the issue of excessive computational cost, we
 integrated a Region of Interest (ROI) processing framework. Instead of analyzing the entire video
 frame, this solution intelligently identifies and isolates only the necessary sections where faces are
 detected. By cutting the frame to process only the relevant pixels, the system could perform its
 analytical tasks with a fraction of the original data. Track Duration Management Protocol: To resolve
 the memory overload caused by long-duration tracks, HumbleBee AI developed and implemented a
 track-timing mechanism. We configured a 30-second timer for each active track. If a track remained
 active beyond this predefined period, the system automatically designated it as removed and assigned
 a new track ID if the person remained in view. This protocol effectively caps the number of frames
 stored for any single track, preventing memory usage from escalating uncontrollably.

 Results
 The implementation of HumbleBee AI's custom solutions delivered immediate and measurable
 improvements to the LumioHub AI platform's performance and efficiency. Drastically Lowered
 Computational Costs: The ROI framework successfully minimized the data requiring analysis, leading
 to faster and more efficient processing. The client's system can now handle a higher volume of video
 streams simultaneously without overburdening its computational resources. Controlled and Optimized
 Memory Usage: The track-timing protocol has completely mitigated the risk of RAM overload. The
 platform maintains stable memory consumption, even when individuals remain visible on camera for
 extended periods, ensuring system reliability and scalability. Enhanced Real-Time Processing: By
 enabling track data to be processed without waiting for a subject to disappear, the solution has
 improved the platform's overall responsiveness. The system can now deliver insights and analytics
 with even lower latency.

 platform remains at the cutting edge of innovation. This collaboration exemplifies our mission: to
