                                                                                   This study demonstrated the effect of feature engineering
                                                                                on the receptivity prediction performance of an ML model.
                                                                                In other words, the results revealed that the same ML model
                                                                                could perform better when richer features were engineered
                                                                                (average ROC-AUC of 0.565 versus 0.547 for CFFs and TFs).
