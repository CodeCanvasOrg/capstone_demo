 Executive Summary
 Johnson and Johnson partnered with HumbleBeeAI to answer a critical question: Do their events
 actually increase medical procedure volumes?. The answer came through a powerful Power BI
 solution. HumbleBeeAI gave J&J the ability to measure event effectiveness, compare regional
 performance, and prove return on investment with confidence by turning fragmented and inconsistent
 data into a unified analytics platform.

 Introduction
 As a global healthcare leader, Johnson and Johnson invests heavily in educational and promotional
 events for medical professionals. These events are designed to strengthen relationships and
 encourage the adoption of J&J products. But for all the resources spent, one challenge remained:
 could the company demonstrate a clear link between events and actual increases in procedure
 volume? To move beyond anecdotal feedback, J&J needed a reliable data driven solution.

 The Problem
 J&J's teams faced major roadblocks in evaluating event performance:

  •   Event data sat in isolation from procedure data, making impact measurement impossible.
  •   There were no benchmarks to compare accounts with events against those without them.
  •   Datasets were riddled with inconsistencies such as missing account codes and formatting issues,
      demanding hours of manual cleanup.
  •   Regional comparisons were limited, leaving decision makers with little clarity on where utilization
      was strongest or weakest.
  •   Any future solution needed to handle thousands of records smoothly while being easy enough for
      non technical users to explore independently.
 Without a unified analytics framework, J&J could not confidently assess whether events were truly
 delivering value.

 The Solution
 HumbleBeeAI built a complete analytics system around Microsoft Power BI. The design focused on
 three pillars.

 1. Data Integration and Preprocessing

 The first challenge was data quality. Using Power Query, HumbleBeeAI cleaned and merged account
 records, event logs, and procedure data. Missing codes were filled, duplicates resolved, and
 inconsistencies corrected. The result was a single reliable dataset that eliminated hours of manual
 effort and created a trustworthy foundation for analysis.

 2. Advanced Event-Impact Analysis

 With clean data, the team introduced multiple analytical perspectives inside Power BI. Procedure
 volumes before and after events were compared to calculate growth at the account level. Benchmarks
 were established by contrasting event hosting accounts with non event accounts. Utilization was
 mapped geographically, giving leaders a clear picture of where events were working and where they
 were not.

 3. Interactive and Scalable Dashboards

 Finally, HumbleBeeAI delivered sleek and dynamic dashboards. Stakeholders could filter by account,
 event type, procedure, or timeframe and immediately see the results. This interactivity allowed them to
 drill into the data at any level, uncover insights quickly, and adapt strategies on the fly without needing
 technical support.

 Result
 The impact of the Power BI solution was transformative:

  •   J&J gained clear visibility into event ROI, identifying accounts where procedure volumes rose
      directly after events.
  •   Leaders could now make data driven decisions, reallocating resources toward high growth regions
      and refining underperforming strategies.
  •   Concrete performance benchmarks proved that accounts hosting events consistently outperformed
      those that did not.
  •   Manual cleaning work was eliminated, saving countless hours and freeing teams to focus on
      strategy.
  •   Data accuracy and reliability improved significantly thanks to structured mapping and consistent
      calculations.
  •   Stakeholders across the company felt empowered by intuitive dashboards that encouraged
      exploration and insight discovery.

 Conclusion
 What began as a fragmented and frustrating data challenge became a powerful competitive
 advantage. Johnson and Johnson now has the ability to validate event ROI with precision, guide
 strategy with reliable benchmarks, and ensure every marketing investment contributes measurable
 value.

 Through this collaboration, HumbleBeeAI demonstrated how structured analytics can transform raw
 information into insights that directly impact business growth. J&J is not only measuring its past events
 more effectively but is also building a scalable platform to guide future strategies with confidence.

 HumbleBeeAI continues to help industry leaders unlock similar value from their data.
