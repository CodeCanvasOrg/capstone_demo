 Executive Summary
 A leading enterprise client struggled to measure its brand visibility on generative AI platforms like
 ChatGPT and Gemini, hindering strategic decision-making. HumbleBee AI developed the 'Generative
 Engine Visibility Dashboard' using Amazon QuickSight, creating a centralized, automated analytics
 platform. This solution transformed the client's manual guesswork into a streamlined, data-driven
 workflow, providing clear, accurate, and convenient insights for marketing, SEO, and product teams.

 Introduction
 In an era where digital presence defines market leadership, our client, a forward-thinking enterprise,
 recognized the emerging importance of generative AI as a new frontier for brand visibility. As platforms
 like ChatGPT and Gemini become primary sources of information for millions, understanding how a
 brand is represented is no longer just an advantage-it's a necessity. However, the client lacked the
 tools to navigate this new landscape, leaving them unable to quantify or influence their standing in this
 critical channel.

 The Problem
 The client faced a significant and multifaceted challenge that impeded their ability to compete
 effectively in the age of AI.

  •   Lack of Measurability: The primary obstacle was the inability to track brand visibility on
      generative AI engines. Without a systematic way to monitor mentions, rankings, and the context of
      their appearances, the client was operating in the dark. This made it impossible to formulate
      data-driven strategies to improve their digital presence on these influential platforms.
  •   Scalability Issues: The volume of data generated by these engines is immense and constantly
      growing. The client needed a solution capable of processing large, unstructured text outputs from
      multiple sources and transforming them into structured, actionable insights. A manual approach
      was not only time-consuming but also prone to error and incapable of scaling.
  •   Poor User Experience for Non-Technical Teams: Any potential solution had to be accessible to
      various departments, including marketing, SEO, and product management. The client required an
      intuitive interface that would allow non-technical users to explore complex data, apply filters, and
      extract meaningful insights without extensive training or reliance on data science teams.
 Without a solution, the client risked falling behind competitors, missing opportunities for brand
 enhancement, and making strategic decisions based on incomplete information rather than empirical
 evidence.

 The Solution
 To address these challenges, HumbleBee AI engineered a powerful and sophisticated data analytics
 solution centered around Amazon QuickSight. We developed the Generative Engine Visibility
 Dashboard, a custom-built platform designed to deliver clear, real-time insights into brand
 performance on generative AI.

 Our approach was comprehensive, leveraging a robust tech stack to ensure automation, scalability,
 and an exceptional user experience.

  1. Automated Data Ingestion and Processing: We utilized Amazon Athena to automatically
      connect to and query data from various sources, including files stored in Amazon S3. This
      eliminated manual data handling and allowed for the on-the-fly processing of unstructured text from
      generative engines. Python scripts were employed for more complex data transformations,
      converting raw text into a structured, quantifiable format.
  2. Advanced Analytics and Metrics: Within Amazon QuickSight, we implemented custom logic
      using calculated fields and Level-Aware Calculations (LAC) to generate meaningful metrics. The
      dashboard provides a real-time view of visibility trends, including brand mention frequency, ranking
      positions, and comparative performance analysis against competitors.

  3. Interactive and Intuitive Visualizations: The solution features a multi-tabbed dashboard with a
      range of interactive charts, graphs, and tables. Key metrics are presented in an easy-to-digest
      format, allowing users to quickly grasp performance at a glance. Dynamic filtering and drill-down
      capabilities empower users to explore the data by specific companies, date ranges, or other
      variables, uncovering granular insights with ease.
  4. Scalable and Robust Architecture: By building the platform on AWS and leveraging the SPICE
      (Super-fast, Parallel, In-memory Calculation Engine) in QuickSight, we ensured the solution
      could handle large and growing data volumes without sacrificing performance. This architecture
      provides the client with a scalable, future-proof platform for their analytics needs.
 The implementation of the Generative Engine Visibility Dashboard empowered the client to move from
 reactive guesswork to a proactive, data-driven strategy.

 Result
 The deployment of the Generative Engine Visibility Dashboard delivered transformative results,
 providing the client with a significant competitive advantage. The project successfully converted a
 manual, inefficient process into a streamlined, automated workflow that provides immediate value
 across the organization.

  •   Data-Driven Decision-Making: The client can now accurately measure and analyze their brand's
      visibility on generative AI platforms. This has enabled them to make informed, strategic decisions
      to enhance their online presence and marketing efforts based on concrete data.
  •   Unprecedented Accuracy: The dashboards offer a clear, data-backed view of brand
      performance. This ensures that strategic planning is based on reliable, precise, and up-to-date
      information, removing ambiguity and increasing confidence in outcomes.
  •   Enhanced Convenience and Collaboration: The user-friendly, interactive interface allows teams
      across marketing, SEO, and product to self-serve their data needs. This accessibility has fostered
      greater collaboration and aligned departmental efforts toward common visibility goals,
      democratizing insights across the organization.
 Ultimately, the solution provided by HumbleBee AI has led to more consistent visibility gains and a
 stronger competitive position for the client in the rapidly evolving digital landscape.

 Conclusion
 The partnership between HumbleBee AI and our client demonstrates the power of harnessing
 specialized AI and data science to solve complex, real-world business problems. By creating the
 Generative Engine Visibility Dashboard, we provided not just a tool but a strategic asset that turns raw
 data into a competitive edge.

 As the influence of generative AI continues to grow, our client is now well-positioned to adapt and
 thrive. Our ongoing partnership will focus on evolving the platform's capabilities, incorporating new
 data sources, and refining the analytical models to ensure sustained success. This project stands as a
 testament to our mission: to deliver exceptional, scalable AI-driven solutions that elevate businesses.
