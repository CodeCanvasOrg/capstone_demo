 Executive Summary
 An Augmented Reality (AR) based company partnered with HumbleBeeAI to solve challenges they
 faced in accurately detecting the center of sports fields using mobile devices. The goal was to create a
 solution that could provide real-time, precise field center detection and alignment, enhancing the user
 experience for AR applications. The solution developed by HumbleBeeAI involved a combination of
 deep learning models, optimized for real-time performance on mobile devices. The app delivers
 accurate field center localization with minimal computational overhead.

 Introduction
 Accurate field center detection is crucial for various applications, including sports broadcasting,
 coaching, and augmented reality experiences. Traditional methods of field alignment often rely on
 manual measurements or expensive equipment, which can be time-consuming and prone to errors.
 Recognizing these challenges, the client sought to develop a mobile application that could
 automatically detect the center of sports fields in real time.

 The Problem
 Traditional field center detection methods present several challenges:

  •   Manual Measurements: Relying on physical markers or manual alignment can be inaccurate and
      time-consuming.
  •   Equipment Costs: High-quality cameras and sensors are often required, increasing the cost of
      implementation.
  •   Environmental Factors: Lighting conditions, weather, and field conditions can affect detection
      accuracy.
  •   Real-Time Requirements: Many applications need instant feedback, which traditional methods
      struggle to provide.
 In a dynamic sports environment, these challenges can lead to inefficiencies and reduced user
 experience, particularly for AR applications that require precise alignment.

 The Solution
 To address these challenges, the company collaborated with HumbleBeeAI to develop a mobile
 application that leverages deep learning for real-time field center detection. The solution combines
 lightweight segmentation models with keypoint estimation techniques, optimized for mobile devices.
 The app allows users to point their phone camera at the sports field, and within seconds, the system
 identifies the field center and key alignment points.

 The solution was designed to operate seamlessly on both iOS and Android devices, providing
 accurate results without the need for internet connectivity. The app also includes features such as
 customizable thresholds, exportable logs, and easy integration with existing mobile applications.

 Results
 The implementation of the Field Center Detection app has led to:

  •   Real-Time Detection: Instant identification of field centers and alignment points.
  •   Increased Accuracy: Reliable detection across various lighting and environmental conditions.
  •   Cost Efficiency: Reduced reliance on expensive equipment and manual labor.
  •   Enhanced User Experience: Improved AR applications with precise field alignment.
  •   Operational Efficiency: Significant reduction in time spent on field alignment tasks.
  •   User Engagement: Enhanced AR experiences for fans and users in sports settings.
