 Executive Summary
 LiveScore AI is a low-latency, real-time scoring platform designed to enhance basketball trick-shot and
 dunk events with animated scores, live leaderboards, and streamlined administrative controls.
 LiveScore AI provides instant feedback to players and spectators, combining player movement
 tracking, trick recognition, and automated scoring in a unified system. By reducing human error and
 increasing audience engagement, the platform offers tournament operators, event producers, and
 venues the tools they need for seamless event execution, while ensuring that results are accurate,
 shareable, and reliable for post-event analysis.

 Introduction
 Basketball events, particularly trick-shot and dunk competitions, rely on real-time feedback and clear,
 transparent outcomes. Traditional methods of scoring often suffer from delays, subjectivity, and errors,
 diminishing the excitement for both players and spectators. LiveScore AI was created to automate and
 streamline the scoring process, integrating scoring, visual animations, and administrative control into a
 unified platform. Designed with scalability in mind, LiveScore AI supports multiple concurrent games
 and real-time event management, offering a seamless solution for interactive sports events.

 The Problem
 Inefficient scoring systems: Traditional scoring methods are manual and prone to human error,
 causing delays in providing feedback to players and audiences. Lack of engagement: In many
 events, players and spectators are disconnected from the scoring process, leading to less excitement
 and engagement. Operational challenges: Event organizers often struggle with managing real-time
 game updates, leaderboard maintenance, and score adjustments without interrupting the event.
 Limited scalability: As events grow in popularity, the need to manage multiple concurrent games with
 minimal latency becomes crucial. Traditional systems cannot support large-scale, real-time event
 scoring without sacrificing accuracy or speed.

 The Solution
 LiveScore AI tackles these challenges with a comprehensive, real-time solution for scoring and event
 management.

 Real-Time Event Ingest & Scoring
 LiveScore AI utilizes real-time data streaming to process scores instantly as the game unfolds,
 updating both player-facing displays and spectator screens without delay. This real-time scoring
 eliminates bottlenecks and enhances audience engagement.

 Automated Scoring Engine
 The platform’s deterministic scoring engine automates the process of recognizing player movements,
 tricks, and dunk events, providing immediate and accurate scores. This reduces the need for manual
 scoring and minimizes errors, ensuring a transparent and fair competition.

 Engaging Spectator UI
 LiveScore AI features a dynamic and animated user interface that displays live trick queues and
 motion choreography, creating an engaging experience for spectators. These animations keep
 audiences invested in the event, helping maintain excitement throughout the competition.

 Edge-Powered Video Processing
 LiveScore AI leverages NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano edge devices to process live video feeds directly
 on-site. The platform supports both RTSP streams from networked CCTV cameras and directly
 connected cameras attached to the device, giving clients the flexibility to choose the input source that
 best fits their venue setup. The system performs real-time video ingestion and inference at the edge,
 eliminating the need to send raw footage to remote servers. The processed video stream is then
 relayed back to the client, providing spectators and administrators with low-latency, live camera feeds
 alongside scoring data. This edge-first architecture minimizes network bandwidth requirements and
 ensures the system remains responsive even in venue environments with limited connectivity.

 Persistent Leaderboards & Analytics
 LiveScore AI offers persistent leaderboards that are automatically updated during the event. These
 leaderboards are accessible for analysis and reporting, allowing organizers to track player
 performance, create rankings, and export results for future use.

 Admin Interface & Control
 Event organizers benefit from a robust administrative interface that allows for score edits, leaderboard
 adjustments, and real-time control of the event. With secure authentication and role-based access,
 administrators can make changes without interrupting gameplay, ensuring smooth event flow.

 Results
 LiveScore AI has transformed how basketball trick-shot and dunk events are managed and scored.
 Key results include:

  •   Increased Engagement: Real-time scoring with dynamic animations boosts audience participation
      and player excitement.
  •   Operational Efficiency: Automated scoring and leaderboard updates reduce the need for manual
      intervention, allowing event organizers to focus on other aspects of the event.
  •   Improved Accuracy: The platform’s automated scoring ensures consistent, fair, and transparent
      adjudication, reducing human error and disputes.
