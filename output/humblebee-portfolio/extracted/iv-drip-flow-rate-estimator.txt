 Introduction
 Accurate IV flow rate monitoring is essential in hospitals to ensure that patients receive the correct
 dosage of fluids or medication. Traditionally, nurses have relied on manual counting of drops, which
 requires continuous attention and can lead to inconsistency, especially in high-pressure environments.
 Recognizing this challenge, the client aimed to create a smart, mobile-based solution that could
 automatically determine IV flow rates using the device's camera. The objective was to deliver fast,
 precise, and real-time measurements while maintaining full patient privacy and reliability in any
 healthcare environment.

 The Problem
 Traditional IV monitoring methods, while simple, present several operational and clinical limitations:

  •   Human Error: Counting drops manually often results in inconsistent readings due to distractions or
      fatigue.
  •   Time-Intensive: Staff must dedicate up to 3 minutes per observation, which is unsustainable when
      managing multiple patients.
  •   Delayed Detection: Problems like air bubbles, occlusions, or incorrect drip speeds may go
      unnoticed for several minutes.
  •   Inconsistent Accuracy: Variations in lighting, visibility, and observer skill levels lead to unreliable
      data.
 In a busy hospital setting, these limitations reduce efficiency, increase cognitive load on nurses, and
 can affect patient care outcomes.

 The Solution
 To address these challenges, the company - in collaboration with HumbleBeeAI - introduced a mobile
 application that estimates IV drip flow rate automatically in real time.

 The app allows healthcare professionals to simply point their phone camera at the IV setup, and within
 seconds, the system displays the estimated flow rate. It eliminates manual counting, ensures
 consistent readings, and provides reliable results across different conditions - without requiring internet
 access.

 The solution was designed to operate seamlessly on both iOS and Android devices, supporting on-site
 staff in hospitals, clinics, and even remote healthcare facilities.

 Results
 The transition from manual to AI flow rate estimation has led to:

  •   Time Savings: Over 10x faster than manual counting.
  •   Higher Reliability: Consistent accuracy independent of human factors.
  •   Improved Patient Safety: Rapid detection of irregularities or stoppages.
  •   Staff Efficiency: Frees up nurses to focus on higher-value clinical tasks.
  •   Operational Flexibility: Works seamlessly in any environment, including low-connectivity or rural
      settings.
  •   Reduced Manual Workload: Nurses save valuable time and mental effort.
