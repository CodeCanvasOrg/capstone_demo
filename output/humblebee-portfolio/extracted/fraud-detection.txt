 Introduction
 The retail technology company provides a comprehensive cloud SaaS platform for distribution. The
 all-in-one platform manages sales and field teams, trade marketing, retail execution, warehouses,
 finance, and analytics for over 600 customers across Central Asia and beyond. Serving distributors
 and manufacturers in FMCG, beverage, pharmaceutical, automotive, and similar verticals, the
 company's platform integrates AI-powered shelf recognition and marking/code scanning as critical
 components of their retail execution capabilities.

 The platform's end users - major players in these industries with products distributed across thousands
 of retail locations - depend on precise in-store execution to maintain their competitive edge. Effective
 visual merchandising, optimal product placement, and consistent promotional campaigns are critical to
 driving sales and reinforcing brand identity. To ensure these standards were met, these corporations
 relied on vast networks of field agents to manually audit product displays, a process that was not only
 costly but also vulnerable to inaccuracies and fraud.

 The Problem
 The company faced significant operational hurdles that undermined the integrity and efficiency of its
 retail compliance strategy. The manual auditing process was plagued by several critical issues:

 Inaccurate and Fraudulent Reporting: Field agents were found to be submitting fraudulent reports,
 including images of device screens, photos of miniature reference displays, and pictures of closed
 coolers. This compromised the validity of the audit data, leading to incorrect assessments of in-store
 conditions.

 Difficulty Monitoring Fraud at Scale: With thousands of retail outlets, manually verifying fraud
 actions was an insurmountable task. This resulted in inconsistent visual merchandising and diluted the
 impact of marketing efforts.

 High Operational Costs: The manual checking of fraud actions was labor-intensive and expensive,
 requiring significant investment in personnel and resources without guaranteeing reliable results.

 Lack of Data-Driven Insights: The inconsistent and often inaccurate data collected from the field
 made it impossible to derive actionable insights, hindering the company's ability to make strategic
 decisions about its retail partnerships and merchandising strategies.

 These challenges created a significant gap between the company's merchandising goals and its actual
 in-store execution, threatening brand positioning and ROI.

 The Solution
 To overcome these challenges, HumbleBeeAI implemented a solution designed to automate and
 monitor agents' fraud actions. This multi-faceted system leveraged cutting-edge Computer Vision and
 AI to deliver unparalleled accuracy and efficiency.

 The core components of the solution included:

 Multi-Layered Fraud Prevention: To combat fraudulent submissions, we integrated a powerful
 anti-fraud engine. This system utilized three distinct detection mechanisms:

  •   Device Screen Detection: A specialized classification model identifies images captured from a
      phone or tablet screen.

 Results
 The implementation of HumbleBeeAI's anti-fraud AI solution delivered immediate and transformative

 Fraudulent submissions were detected with over 90% accuracy within the first week, increasing

 The system's ability to process a massive volume of image inputs from thousands of locations
 automated an unfeasible task to evaluate manually, ensuring consistent visual merchandising
 across the entire retail network.

 By providing clean, validated data, the platform enabled the company to make data-driven decisions,
 leading to optimized merchandising strategies, stronger retail partnerships, and maximized return on
