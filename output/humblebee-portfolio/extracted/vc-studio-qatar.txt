 HumbleBeeAI is pleased to announce the establishment of HumbleBeeAI Venture Studio LLC in the
 Qatar Financial Centre (QFC), marking an important step in the company’s international growth.

 The new Qatar entity has been created to serve as HumbleBeeAI’s Middle East ve nture-building hub,
 supporting the launch, incubation, and scaling of future AI-driven ventures across the region.

 Purpose of HumbleBeeAI Venture Studio LLC
 The Qatar entity has been established not only as a local company, but as a venture studio designed
 to:

  •   Launch and support new AI startups and ventures in Qatar
  •   Explore partnerships with local organizations, investors, universities, and government programs

 Why Qatar
 Qatar is becoming one of the region’s most promising destinations for technology, AI, and startup
 growth. Through initiatives led by QFC, Invest Qatar, Qatar Development Bank, and other national
 programs, the country is actively supporting international founders and innovative companies.

 HumbleBeeAI believes Qatar offers a unique opportunity to:

  •   Expand into the Middle East market
  •   Collaborate with local and international partners
  •   Access a fast-growing innovation ecosystem
  •   Build ventures that can scale regionally and globally

 Looking Ahead
 Over the coming months, HumbleBeeAI Venture Studio LLC plans to:

  •   Complete its operational setup in Qatar
  •   Build relationships with local partners and stakeholders
  •   Explore office, residency, and investment opportunities
  •   Introduce HumbleBeeAI’s existing products and future ventures to the Qatar market
  •   Seek collaboration with investors, strategic partners, universities, and government initiatives
 The establishment of HumbleBeeAI Venture Studio LLC reflects HumbleBeeAI’s long-term vision:
 building globally connected, impact-driven AI ventures that create value across multiple regions.

 We look forward to beginning this new chapter in Qatar.
