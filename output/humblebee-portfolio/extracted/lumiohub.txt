 Executive Summary
 LumioHub AI is a modern workplace management platform that combines real-time sensing,
 intelligent automation, and enterprise-grade analytics to improve attendance accuracy,
 strengthen on-premise security, and reduce administrative workload. Built for multi-site
 organizations and service providers, it supports secure multi-tenant deployments with strict data
 separation and configurable governance controls.

 Beyond basic check-in and check-out, LumioHub AI provides continuous workplace insights,
 including desk presence tracking, live indoor location awareness, break and wellbeing reminders,
 fatigue and rest detection, interaction and conversation signals, focus or distraction indicators (such
 as phone usage during defined work windows), and role-based performance reporting. All insights
 are delivered through dashboards, summaries, spatial views, and exportable reports, enabling
 operational visibility without the need for manual supervision or continuous video monitoring.

 Introduction
 Organizations still rely on manual attendance, access cards, spreadsheets, and ad-hoc supervision to
 manage workforce operations. These approaches often lead to:

  •   Inaccurate records and time fraud (buddy punching, missed entries)
  •   Delayed security response due to limited real-time visibility
  •   High administrative overhead for HR and operations
  •   Limited insight into how time is spent across teams, roles, and locations
  •   Inconsistent policy enforcement across departments and sites
 LumioHub AI was designed to address these issues through an integrated platform that turns
 workplace activity into structured, actionable operations data. The system supports:

  •   Automated, identity-based attendance tracking and office-hours calculation
  •   Real-time on-premise security monitoring with alerts for suspicious or unrecognized presence
  •   Live indoor localization tracking and map-based visualization
  •   Desk presence analytics to understand working patterns and session-based activity rhythms
  •   Detection of prolonged interactions or conversations between individuals
  •   Configurable break reminders to reduce prolonged continuous sitting and support healthier work
      habits
  •   Optional phone usage detection for selected roles or teams within defined time windows
  •   Fatigue and sleeping detection based on prolonged inactivity or posture patterns, with configurable
      tolerance thresholds
  •   Centralized dashboards and reports for individuals, teams, departments, and locations

 Scope and Capabilities
 LumioHub AI operates as an end-to-end workplace operations platform, covering four core areas:

 1) Workforce Attendance and Timekeeping
  •   Automated check-in and check-outusing designated entry and exit points
  •   Consolidated office-hours calculationwith protection against duplicate or inconsistent records
  •   Daily, weekly, and monthly summarieswith historical trend tracking
  •   Configurable rules and thresholds (e.g., highlighting underperformance such as fewer than defined
      weekly hours)
  •   Consistent attendance policies across departments, locations, and tenants

 2) On-Premise Security and Access Visibility
  •   Continuous monitoringof entry points, shared spaces, and controlled areas
  •   Detection and alertingfor unrecognized or unauthorized individuals
  •   Identification of suspicious access patterns, including tailgating risk indicators
  •   Evidence-ready event logging to support incident review and compliance reporting
  •   Site-level and tenant-level configuration for access rules and alert routing
